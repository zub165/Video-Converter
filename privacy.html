Media Converter Pro (published by Innovators Generation) is a privacy-first media conversion app. Audio and video conversion runs locally on your device.

Data we collect

  • Anonymous user ID: A random identifier stored on your device and sent over HTTPS only for optional preference sync and delete requests. It is not linked to your name, email, or phone.
  • App settings: Theme, language, quality preset, and notification preference may sync to our backend over HTTPS when online.
  • Media files you select: Processed on your device only. Files are not uploaded for conversion.
  • Conversion history: Stored locally on your device only (not uploaded).

How we use data

Collected data is used for app functionality only (save settings, process delete requests). We do not sell data, show ads, or use data for tracking.

Hybrid storage

Primary storage is on your device. Optional HTTPS endpoints at citizen-api.mywaitime.com handle preferences and deletion requests only — never your media files.

Delete your data

In Media Converter Pro: Settings → Delete data. You can delete all data, history only, reset settings, or clear cache.

Web instructions: Delete Data. Email: zub165@yahoo.com (subject: Delete My Data).

Media Converter Pro is not directed at children under 13.
