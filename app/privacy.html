Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Media Converter Pro ("we", "our", or "the app") is privacy-first. Media conversion runs locally on your device.

Data We Collect

  • Files you select: Processed locally. Files are not uploaded for conversion.
  • Conversion history: Stored locally (SQLite / SharedPreferences) with optional HTTPS backend sync.
  • App settings: Theme, quality preset, and preferences on device; optional secure storage for user profile.

Hybrid Storage

Primary storage is on your device. Optional backend endpoints use HTTPS for preferences and deletion requests only.

Delete Your Data

Use Settings → Delete data / account in the app, or follow Delete Data.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13.
