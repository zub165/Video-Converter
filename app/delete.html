Delete Your Data

You can remove conversion history, reset settings, clear cache, or delete all local data.

In the app

  1. Open Media Converter Pro
  2. Go to Settings → Delete data / account
  3. Choose: Delete all, Delete history, Reset settings, or Clear cache
  4. Confirm and execute

By email

Email privacy@mediaconverter.app with subject Delete My Data. We process requests within 30 days.

What is removed

  • Local conversion history
  • Preferences and secure profile storage
  • Temporary cache files
  • Optional remote preference / account records via HTTPS delete APIs

Media files you converted remain in your device storage unless you delete them manually.
