This page explains how to delete data collected by Media Converter Pro, published by Innovators Generation. No sign-in is required.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Media Converter Pro on your device
  2. Go to Settings (bottom navigation)
  3. Tap Delete data / account
  4. Choose one option:
    • Delete all — removes local history, settings, cache, and requests remote preference deletion
    • Delete history — removes conversion history only
    • Reset settings — restores default preferences
    • Clear cache — removes temporary files
  5. Confirm your choice

Delete by email

Email zub165@yahoo.com with subject Delete My Data - Media Converter Pro. Include your platform (Android/iOS/Web). We process requests within 30 days.

What is removed

  • Local conversion history and metadata
  • App preferences (theme, language, quality preset)
  • Anonymous user ID and secure local profile storage
  • Temporary cache files
  • Optional remote preference records (HTTPS delete API)

What is not removed automatically

Converted audio/video files saved to your device storage remain until you delete them manually in your file manager or Photos app.
